# @agent-evals/verdict

Turns run records into a comparison of two arms and one verdict: better, worse, or not conclusive.

## Owns

- `src/cells.ts`: `matchCells`, which groups scores by task for the two arms.
- `src/compare.ts`: `compareArms`, which picks the design, and runs the methods.
- `src/sample.ts`: `UnitSample`, the units of one comparison, with its estimate and its redraw for a simulation.
- `src/verdict.ts`: `decideVerdict`.
- `src/power.ts`: `planPower`, which simulates the verdict rule to find the units needed.
- `src/simulation.ts`: synthetic experiments for tests, exported as `@agent-evals/verdict/testing`.

## Rules

- The task is the unit of analysis. With one task, the unit is the run, and the comparison says so.
- A verdict of better or worse needs a p-value below alpha and an interval that excludes zero.
- `src/simulation.test.ts` holds the false positive rate at or below alpha. A change to a method must keep it green.
